👀 F.R.E.E.D.O.M

A floating emoji ball that keeps an eye on your screen and answers whatever you are looking at: questions, errors, code, forms.

  1. Tap the ball and paste your Anthropic API key (stored only in this browser).
  2. Choose what it watches: 📺 Screen (share your entire screen), 📷 Camera (point your phone at any screen or page), or 🖼️ Photo (a screenshot).
  3. Leave it running. When something needs an answer, the ball turns 💡.

💻 Laptop / desktop browser

Use 📺 Screen and pick Entire screen. In Chrome or Edge, press ⧉ Pop out to float the ball above every other app.

📱 Phone / tablet

Phone browsers can't read other apps' screens, so use 📷 Camera or 🖼️ Photo. Add this page to your home screen to open it like an app.

🖥️ Desktop app

The F.R.E.E.D.O.M desktop app (Windows, macOS, Linux) floats over every app, watches all the time, and answers on Ctrl+Shift+Space. See the README.

Every look sends one picture of your screen to the Claude API using your key. Pause the ball any time with ⏸. Nothing is stored on any server by this app.

F.R.E.E.D.O.M ready

Saved only in this browser and sent only to api.anthropic.com. Get a key at console.anthropic.com.
